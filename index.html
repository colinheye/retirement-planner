About this model

This calculator runs thousands of randomized simulations of your financial future. Each one rolls the dice on stock returns, inflation, healthcare costs, and how long you and your spouse live — then tracks your accounts, taxes, income, and spending year by year.

The Success Rate is the percentage of simulations where your liquid assets stayed above the Minimum Liquid Buffer all the way through end of life.

What to aim for: 80–90% is generally considered a comfortable target — high enough that failure is unlikely, but not so high that you're hoarding money you could be enjoying. Above 95% often means you're being overly conservative and could safely spend more. Below 70% leaves little room for surprises. Plans should be revisited as life unfolds, and spending kept flexible.

Adjust the inputs below and tap Run Simulation. Your inputs save automatically in this browser.

All values are in today's dollars — the simulation inflates them internally each year, so you don't have to project future prices yourself.
